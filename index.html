About the Project

How this AI-powered glossary was developed.

Why legal terminology?

Legal translation requires precise terminology because the same concept can have established equivalents in different legal systems. The glossary focuses on common terms useful for translation students.

AI tools used

  • ChatGPT — drafting definitions, examples and initial translations.
  • AI-assisted proofreading — checking clarity and consistency.
  • Web research — verifying terminology against authoritative legal resources.

Verification process

AI-generated content was reviewed manually. Translations were compared with terminology used in official Kazakhstan legal documents and legal information resources. Inaccurate or overly literal wording was corrected.

Website structure

The site contains a Home section, searchable Glossary, About the Project section and Sources. The responsive design works on both desktop and mobile screens.

Terminology was checked using official legal texts, bilingual legal documents and general dictionary resources where appropriate. AI was used as an assisting tool, not as the only source of verification.
